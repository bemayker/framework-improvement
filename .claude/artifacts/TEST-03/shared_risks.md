## Potential conflicts with other independent features
- backend/app/main.py may also be modified by TEST-02 (health endpoint, independent, could run concurrently): both register a router in the app factory, exactly the collision `feature_map.md` flags for this pair; serialize TEST-02 and TEST-03 rather than running them concurrently. TEST-02's build would also collide on backend/tests/conftest.py if it introduces the DB fixtures its comments promise, since TEST-03 now creates them first.
- frontend/src/components/LandingPage.tsx may also be modified by TEST-04 (page footer, independent per the graph): flagged in `feature_map.md`, but TEST-04's footer is already present on main (AppFooter is rendered by LandingPage), so this conflict is historical unless TEST-04 is revised concurrently.
- backend/app/main.py may also be modified by TEST-05 (version endpoint, independent per the graph): `feature_map.md` flags TEST-05 against TEST-02 on this file, and TEST-03 touches it too; TEST-05's version router is already registered on main, so this conflict is historical unless TEST-05 is revised concurrently.
